### App Store Description
Every climb leaves a profile. Ridgeline gives hikers a focused place to preserve it.

Log an ascent with its date, distance, elevation gain, duration, route, gear, and personal notes. Your dashboard shapes those entries into a distinctive ridge profile, creating an immediate view of the effort behind your days outside.

Go beyond a simple activity list:

- Compare elevation gain across ascents
- Explore pace, grade, and activity distribution
- Review personal records
- Find patterns in Insights
- Return to any entry through History
- Edit or delete records whenever needed

The custom dark topographic interface and subtle parallax effects were built for an immersive trail-inspired experience without getting in the way of fast entry.

Ridgeline is a manual journal, not a tracking service. It does not use GPS, HealthKit, maps, or GPX files. It works offline, requires no account, and keeps all routes, notes, and activity records on your device.

No ads. No analytics. No subscriptions or in-app purchases. No cloud storage.
